# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- **Default set in a group.** New setting in the minimap settings window: choose whether the menu starts on raid markers or ground markers while you're in a party or raid. Joining a group switches to your choice, and the 5-minute idle reset returns to it. On your own it's always raid markers.

## 1.2.1

- **Ground markers need a group.** Ground markers only work in a party or raid, so when you're on your own the swap key does nothing and the menu stays on raid markers. Leaving a group switches the menu back to raid markers.

## 1.2.0

### New

- **Minimap button.** Click the Skull button on the minimap to open a small settings window. Drag it to move it around the minimap. It works with minimap-button collectors such as Leatrix Plus.
- **Settings window:**
  - Shows the key bound to the menu. **Change keybind** opens the game's keybinding settings straight to "Quick Raid Mark Menu".
  - **Raid/ground swap key:** choose Ctrl, Alt or Shift for switching between raid and ground markers. Your choice is saved.
- **Ground markers look different.** On ground markers the menu turns dark brown, and each marker sits on a glowing ground circle in its colour, so you can tell the two sets apart at a glance.

### Changed

- **Clear after number keys.** On ground markers, after placing markers with the number keys, releasing over the clear slot still removes all ground markers. Releasing anywhere else just closes the menu.

## 1.1.0

- **Number keys.** 1-9 mark the unit under the mouse and keep the menu open, so you can mark a whole pack in one hold. 9 clears.
- **Back to raid markers.** The menu returns to raid markers after 5 minutes without use.
- **Quick tap.** A quick tap for Skull skips the selection pulse.
- **Opening animation.** New opening animation.
- **Keybinding renamed.** It's now "Quick Raid Mark Menu". Existing binds keep working.

## 1.0.0

- First release: radial raid and ground marker menu at the cursor.
